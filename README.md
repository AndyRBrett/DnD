# D&D Table Live

A companion for our in-person D&D campaign. The DM speaks into one microphone on the host laptop; the host browser transcribes and publishes updates after pauses. Players join on their phones over the same Wi-Fi, pick their character, and see the current scene, earlier updates, their abilities, and two suggested actions.

The two suggestions come from local rules in `src/scene-options.js`. They only suggest abilities recorded on the character's sheet, list `check` abilities without suggesting them, and leave outcomes to the DM. Players can also copy a prompt with their character and recent scenes into their own AI chat.

### Optional: Ask Claude

If the host laptop has an Anthropic API key, players get an **Ask Claude about this scene** button at the top of the Moves tab, above the local suggestions. Create a `.env` file in the project folder (it is gitignored):

```
ANTHROPIC_API_KEY=sk-ant-...
```

The terminal shows `Claude suggestions · on` at startup. Nothing is sent until a player taps the button. Each tap sends that character's sheet, the session's DM updates, and a short table snapshot from the phone (round and whose turn it is, the character's HP, AC, conditions and what's left of their slots and uses, the party's HP, and enemies only as Healthy, Bloodied, or Down) to Anthropic, and returns the bigger picture plus three options and a question for the DM. Claude is told to recommend only abilities that are ready on the sheet and not spent, never `check` lines, and never to decide outcomes. Each character can ask once every 8 seconds, and a room is capped at 500 requests. It uses `claude-sonnet-5-5` at medium effort. Without a key, the button stays hidden and the app calls no AI model.

## Layout

The app uses the UFC Picked Fight visual system: near-black cards, red primary and gold secondary, Barlow and Barlow Condensed type (SIL OFL, `src/fonts/`), and a fixed bottom tab bar. Inside a table:

- **Scene:** the DM's current scene, *Since then* (players' actions, newest first), earlier updates, and *What is <character> doing?* (the button says who you're posting as; a counter appears near the 1000-character limit, `supabase/migrations/0016_dnd_longer_actions.sql`). On the DM's phone, posts that arrived while an update was being written stay in *Since then*, marked "posted while you were writing", until the next update. The DM's *Transcript and manual updates* box remembers whether it was open.
- **Moves ("What can I do?"):** quick ideas from your sheet first (labeled *From your sheet*), then Ask Claude underneath (labeled *Claude reads the whole story*). When Claude is off, one line says so instead of a missing tab.
- **Sheet:** roll tiles (tap one to roll it), the full character sheet, D&D basics, and copy-to-AI-chat.
- **Combat** (only during a fight): initiative order, whose turn it is, and enemies as Healthy, Bloodied, or Down.
- **DM** (host only): microphone, manual updates, the encounter tracker, actions for each character, character sheet editor, campaign history, and end table.

**Editing the story:** the DM can tap ✎ on anything in the story (the current scene, *Since then*, and earlier updates, including players' actions) to fix it or delete it in place. Players can do the same for their own actions. Edits clear Claude's cached suggestions so the next answer reads the corrected story (`supabase/migrations/0005_dnd_story_edits.sql`). The campaign history itself is edited in the DM tab.

**First visit:** a player's first table on a phone shows a four-step "How it works" walkthrough (pick your character, watch the Scene, fights, where help is); **?** in the header shows it again. The Sheet tab opens *D&D basics* and folds the long character sheet until the player chooses otherwise (remembered per phone). The sign-in screen explains the email code (it comes from UFC Picks, the shared login service) and, from a table link, that you'll go straight to the table.

**Themes:** each phone picks a look under **Theme** on the Sheet tab (or on the home screen): **Arena** (dark red and gold, the default), **Parchment** (light, like an old map), **Arcane** (midnight blue and violet), or **Wildwood** (forest green and amber). `src/theme.js` applies the saved one before the page draws; colors are tokens in `src/style.css` (`:root[data-theme=…]`).

Character chips under the header switch characters. A red dot on Scene means the DM posted something while you were on another tab.

**Dice** (`src/dice.js`): the **Roll** button opens a dice tray (starting on a plain d20, with one-tap chips for your character's rolls and proficient saves, so nothing has to be typed) with d4–d100, a modifier, and advantage or disadvantage (every roll starts on Normal, except one the app sets, such as Reckless or Logan's Feral Instinct initiative). With advantage or disadvantage the d20 that doesn't count is greyed out and captioned "not used". Dice are real 3D solids drawn on a canvas (`src/dice3d.js`: tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, d10 trapezohedron, dodecahedron, icosahedron; d100 is a tens die plus a units die) that tumble, bounce, and land with the rolled number facing you. A natural 20 lands gold, a natural 1 dark (no animation when the phone asks for reduced motion). Tapping a roll tile on the Sheet opens it already filled in, such as `d20+9`. On the hosted version a player can tick *Share with the table* to post the roll to *Since then*; a roll from the plain tray asks what it's for first (Attack, Stealth, Saving throw… or typed), so it posts as "🎲 Stealth (d20): 14". The DM's rolls stay private.

**Conditions** (Scene tab): each player marks what's affecting their character (Poisoned, Prone, Frightened, Exhaustion levels, Concentrating, and the rest of the 5e conditions). The card explains each one in plain words, badges show under the character chips on every tab, the dice tray reminds you when a condition changes your roll, and each change is posted to *Since then* so the DM sees it. Conditions are kept on the player's phone.

**Combat** (`supabase/migrations/0004_dnd_combat.sql`): when the DM taps **Start combat** in the encounter tracker, a Combat tab opens on every phone. It shows the round, whose turn it is, who's up next, and one initiative order with the party and the enemies. Players roll initiative there (with their sheet bonus) or type what they rolled at the table; the DM can fill one in for anyone. The DM moves the turn with **Next turn** and **Back** (downed enemies are skipped, and a new round starts at the top). Players see enemies only as Healthy, Bloodied, or Down, plus their conditions. Exact HP, AC, and attacks never leave the DM's phone: the database function keeps only those public fields. The player whose turn it is gets a gold "Your turn!" banner and a vibration, and an **End my turn** button that passes the turn through the database (`supabase/migrations/0009_dnd_pass_turn.sql`, or the local server), so it works even when the DM's phone is asleep; it uses the same order as the app (highest initiative first, the party first on ties, downed enemies skipped, a new round after the last). Before 0009 runs, it asks the DM's phone with a "⏭️ ends their turn" story line and says so if nothing happens. Only the player whose turn it is can pass it; the DM's Next turn and Back still work anytime. A character who isn't in the fight (on the other side of a door, say) can be sat out: the DM taps **Sit out** on their row, and they drop out of the order, get no turn and no initiative prompt (their phone says they're sitting out), and if it was their turn it moves on. **Join fight** brings them back to roll initiative (`supabase/migrations/0018_dnd_sit_out.sql`, which also makes End my turn skip them; the local server does the same).

**Party HP** (`supabase/migrations/0006_dnd_party_hp.sql`): each character's current, max, and temp HP, shared with the whole table. A ❤️ badge under the character chips shows yours on every tab; the Sheet tab (and the Combat tab during a fight) has your Hit Points card with Damage and Heal; the DM tab has Party HP for everyone; the combat order shows each character's HP. Damage uses temp HP first, healing stops at max, and at 0 a character shows as Down (death saves). Max HP starts from the sheet ("Start at full", or **Everyone to full HP** on the DM's Party HP card), and **Full heal (long rest)** resets it. Leaving Current blank when setting HP means full. The Hit Points card also has the six ability modifiers (tap one to roll a check, or its save — proficient saves in gold) and the combat numbers from the sheet (AC, initiative, speed, passive Perception, save DC, proficiency); an AC the DM confirms there replaces the sheet's (an unsure sheet AC is a range with a ?, such as 18–20?, everywhere: the DM's target list, the TV board, and Ask Claude; an enemy roll inside the range is left to the DM). If a character marked Concentrating loses HP from any source (their own card, the DM's Apply, an enemy attack), their phone shows the Constitution save DC (10 or half the damage) and posts it to the story so the DM sees it. HP is kept per campaign, so it carries from one session to the next; a practice table keeps its own. Damage entered on someone else's card (the DM's Apply, an enemy hit) posts "🩸 takes 12 damage" for that character, which is how their phone asks for one concentration save per hit. Death saves are shared too (they ride in the character's resources record), so the DM's Party HP card shows "Down — death saves · 1 ✓ 2 ✗", damage the DM applies to a downed character counts a failure there, and any healing clears them. The DM's Party HP card also says who's at the table: each player's phone checks in every 20 seconds with the character it's playing (`supabase/migrations/0015_dnd_presence.sql`, or the local server), so the DM sees "At the table: Logan, Arcturus · not here yet: Roan" before anyone posts.

**Combat calculator:** on the DM's enemy cards, pick who the enemy attacks (🎯 Target). An attack roll is checked against that character's AC (a natural 20 is a critical hit with doubled damage dice, a natural 1 misses), and a hit offers **Apply N to <character>** (or **Half** for resistance), which takes it off their shared HP. A multiattack such as "Longsword (×2)" rolls every swing in one tap, each with its own Apply. The built-in stat blocks include tougher foes for a level 10 party (Gladiator, Troll, Hill Giant, Wraith, Oni, Stone Giant, Assassin, Young Green Dragon) and every enemy has saving throw bonuses (from the stat block, or typed on the form as "Str +4, Dex +2"): a save spell in **Hits to apply** has **Roll Dex save (+2)**, which rolls the enemy's save against the spell's DC and applies full, half, or no damage. What the DM applies (and each save) goes to a **To narrate** list on the DM tab; tap a line to drop it into the update box. Players attack from the Combat tab's **Attack** card: pick one of their own attacks (from `attacks` in each sheet in `src/campaign.js`, e.g. Greataxe +8 · 1d12+4, Longbow +11 · 1d8+5, the White Wizard's spell attacks and save spells, or one they added under **Add your own attack**: name, to hit, damage, kept on that phone, for characters like Roan without a full sheet), switch on options (`attackOptions`: Raging, Reckless Attack for advantage, Great Weapon Master, Hunter's Mark, a maneuver die…; each character's options are kept on the phone through character switches and reloads, a one-attack option such as the Dread Ambusher shot or a maneuver turns off after that attack, Reckless ends with the character's turn, and Hunter's Mark spends one 1st-level slot when first ticked and stays up while the ranger is Concentrating), pick the enemy, and **Roll to hit** with the 3D dice. Options the DM hasn't approved yet (Great Weapon Master, Dueling, Hunter's Mark) say "(DM OK?)". A maneuver asks which one (Trip, Goading, Menacing…), and the hit report names it with its save, e.g. "+ Trip Attack (DC 16 Strength save or knocked prone)". Weapon attacks count against Extra Attack: "Attack 2 of 2 in this Attack action". The card shows the math with the ability modifier, e.g. "To hit: d20 + STR +4 + Prof +4 = +8 · Damage: 1d12 + STR +4 + Raging +3" (each attack's `ability` in the sheet; a test checks every listed bonus and DC adds up). A natural 20 is a critical hit (damage dice doubled, plus Logan's Brutal Critical die), a natural 1 misses, otherwise the DM calls Hit or Miss. Then **Roll damage** with the right dice and **Send** it to the DM; save spells (Fireball, Sacred Flame…) skip the to-hit roll and can be sent to several enemies. **Other damage** sends a number without a roll (with **Use last roll**); it goes into the story as "🎯 hit Bandit 2 for 14 damage" and shows on the DM's Combat tab under **Hits to apply** with Apply and Dismiss (plus the to-hit total checked against the enemy's AC, and **Saved: half** for save spells). Players never see enemy HP. At 0 HP a player's card shows **death saves** (three successes, three failures, Roll death save: 10+ succeeds, a 1 is two failures, a 20 brings them back with 1 HP); each roll is posted for the DM, the card shows "Recorded · Rolled 12: success" and the tray closes, and damage entered on your own card while down counts a failure. When every enemy is down, the DM's Combat tab offers **End combat**.

**Resources** (Sheet tab, and the Combat tab during a fight): spell slots by level and limited features from each sheet's `resources` (Rage, Channel Divinity, Warding Flare, superiority dice, Action Surge, Second Wind, Indomitable), shown as dots you tap to spend or get back, with **Short rest** and **Long rest** (a long rest also restores full HP); characters without limited features still get the rest buttons. Counted items that rests don't refill (`per: "never"`: the White Wizard's 3 healing potions, Logan's 4 javelins) show as dots too; drinking a potion from **Healing** spends one. **Healing** on the same card rolls and applies healing in one tap: the sheet's `heals` (Healing Word spends a slot and can target anyone, Second Wind spends its use and heals Dum Dum), a Potion of Healing for anyone, and **Spend a Hit Die** (from the sheet's Hit Dice, d10 + Con each; a long rest gives back half). Each heal is posted to the story. "How spell slots work" explains slots in plain words. Casting a leveled spell from the Attack card spends its slot (pick a higher slot to upcast; Fireball and Guiding Bolt add their extra dice), Radiance of the Dawn spends Channel Divinity, and a maneuver spends a superiority die; with nothing left, the card says so. **Start rage** spends a rage (it ends when the DM taps End combat or on any rest), shows a 😤 Raging badge, switches on Raging (+3) in attacks, and adds a **½ (rage)** damage button; starting a rage and resting are posted to the story for the DM. Resources are shared through the room (`supabase/migrations/0007_dnd_resources.sql`; the local server keeps them too), kept per campaign like HP (a practice table keeps its own), and shown on the DM's Party HP card; until 0007 runs, each phone keeps its own copy and uploads it once the server can hold it. Anything out of uses (a leveled spell with no slot of its level or higher, Rage at 0, maneuvers with no superiority dice…) is left out of the Moves suggestions and sent to Ask Claude as **SPENT**, so neither suggests it until a rest.

**TV board:** anyone at the table can open it: the **📺** button in the header (or the DM tab's **📺 Open the TV board** link) opens the table link plus `&board=1`, a full-screen stage for a screen the whole table can see. Nothing scrolls:
- **The DM's post** plays a passage at a time, like captions: big centered text that fades in, with dots for the passages. It advances at reading pace and rests on the last; arrow keys or the space bar page by hand.
- **Moments:** a strip of the last four since that post, such as "⚔️ Logan hits Vault Dealer for 10", "🎲 Dum Dum · Dexterity check · 17 ✓", or "⏳ Waiting on Roan · Attack roll · AC 12".
- **The party:** portraits with HP rings and AC.
- **In a fight:** a turn track runs across the top, the turn is called out over the stage, and the one acting glows. Enemies show only as Healthy, Bloodied or Down, even when the DM's account opens it.
- **Big moments** flash full-screen: a natural 20 or 1, a critical hit, an enemy falling, a hero going down, Roll initiative, and Victory.

Embers drift in the background; they and the animations stop when the screen asks for reduced motion. The TV's browser signs in once (any player's account, or the DM's).

**Encounter tracker** (`src/encounter.js`, DM tab): **Quick add** puts a group of stock foes in with one tap (47 standard 5e stat blocks with challenge ratings, grouped as Minions, Soldiers, Elites and Bosses for the party's level; enemies added mid-fight roll their own initiative). Each enemy has **Run turn**: its whole routine (two rakes, a dragon's claws and bite) at the picked target, clear hits applied, anything that needs a ruling (rage, an unsure AC, a rider's save) left with its buttons, and one line to the table. Or add enemies from standard 5e stat blocks or your own (name, how many, AC, HP as a number or dice rolled per enemy, initiative, speed, two attacks, abilities), and save your own as templates. It shows how many are still standing, HP bars with damage and heal (bloodied at half, down at 0), attack buttons that roll to-hit and damage (dice doubled on a natural 20), initiative order, and conditions on each enemy. On the hosted app the encounter is saved with the table (`supabase/migrations/0008_dnd_encounter.sql`, host-only functions), so the DM can switch between phone and laptop mid-session; a change this device hasn't finished saving always wins over the table's copy when the page comes back into view (no clock comparison, so a phone whose clock is off can't undo the DM's changes); it's never shown to players, and it goes away when the table ends. On the local server it stays on the DM's device.

## Hosted version (how the group uses it)

**https://andyrbrett.github.io/DnD/**: open it on any phone, sign in with your email, and play. No laptop or shared Wi-Fi needed.

- **Sign in** with an email code. It's the same account system as the UFC Picks app, and the code email comes from UFC Picks.
- **DM:** tap **Start a table**, then **Start listening**, then **Share player link** to send it to the group chat. Whichever account starts a table is its host on any device. Your open tables are listed on the home screen, so you can switch phone ↔ laptop or reload without losing control. A reload shows the table as this phone last saw it straight away ("CATCHING UP" in the corner) while the first request goes out, instead of a blank page. Renaming a character in **Edit character sheets** asks first, because the full sheet, HP, and spell slots follow the name.
- **Players:** open the link, sign in, pick your character. **Ask Claude** works for everyone and uses the Anthropic key already saved in Supabase. Limits: 1 request per character every 8 seconds, 500 per table, 120 per person per day, 600 a day in total. During a fight, **💡 What should I do this turn?** on the Combat tab asks Claude for this turn's options. Ask Claude uses `claude-sonnet-5-5` (roughly $0.04–0.06 a tap).
- **DM:** two Claude tools on the DM tab, for the table's host only and under the same limits (counted once a tap; 1 every 8 seconds for the DM). **✨ Describe enemies** in the encounter tracker turns "three masked croupiers with brass rakes, a hard fight" into stat blocks sized for the party, to check and add in one tap (or save as templates). The **DM assistant** answers a question or a preset (a twist, an NPC, a description, enemy tactics, hints when the players are stuck) from the campaign, this session and the fight as the DM sees it, with lines to add to the next update.
- **Campaign history:** the host opens **Campaign history** on their table to paste notes or add .txt/.md files. Ask Claude reads the whole history before answering, so it knows earlier sessions, names, and open threads. It still suggests only abilities on the character's sheet. When you end a table, you can add that session's updates to the history. Every save keeps the previous version (the last 30) in `dnd_campaign_versions`. The history is cached on Anthropic's side for an hour, so repeat questions are cheap. The limit is 400,000 characters (about 65,000 words).
- **Character sheet:** stats across the top, then Attacks, Spells by level, Features & traits, Skills, and Table moves, with unconfirmed items under *Confirm with the DM first*. Each spell heading says what it means in one line ("Cantrips are simple spells you can cast as often as you like: they never use a spell slot…", and for leveled spells, which slots they use); *D&D basics* defines Cantrip too. Characters with a full sheet in `src/campaign.js` (`sheets`: the White Wizard, Arcturus, Logan, and Dum Dum) also show ability scores, combat stats, saves, all 18 skills, spellcasting, magic items, a first-turn routine, and open questions for the DM. Tap any entry for a built-in plain-language rule (`src/rules.js`, standard 5e summaries) plus your table's note. No AI call. *D&D basics* explains the actions everyone has on their turn (Dash, Dodge, Help, Grapple, concentration, advantage…).
- **Ask Claude:** tap **Ask Claude about this scene**, or type a question (*"Can I cast Silence with one arm held?"*). Claude answers (it takes about a minute; keep playing), gives its read of the bigger picture, and suggests three options. If the scene moves on while Claude is writing, the answer still shows, marked as written for the earlier update. Each option explains how the move works (action or bonus action, range, rolls, cost) and has an editable line you can add to the story.
- **Player actions:** under *What can I do?*, each player types (or dictates) what their character is doing and taps **Add to story**. The host also gets **Actions for each character** (one box per character) for players without a phone out. Everyone sees it under the current scene, Ask Claude reads it, and it's saved into the campaign history with the session (`DM:` and `<Character>:` lines in order).
- **Practice table:** works like a normal table but never adds anything to the campaign history. It shows a PRACTICE label for everyone. Use it to test without touching the story.
- **Export for ChatGPT** (in Campaign history): downloads one .txt file with the history, the current character sheets (ready versus check-with-the-DM), and this table's updates, and also copies it. Attach the file in a ChatGPT thread to continue there. To bring a ChatGPT recap back, paste it into Campaign history and save.
- Tables close after 3 days with no activity (no update, action, shared roll, or change to the fight; `supabase/migrations/0017_dnd_longer_idle.sql`), so a long session is never cut off. **End room and delete session** removes everything right away.

How it's built: GitHub Pages serves the files in `src/` (`.github/workflows/pages.yml` runs the tests, then publishes on every push to `main`). Rooms, logins, and the campaign history live in the UFC app's Supabase project, in tables prefixed `dnd_` (`supabase/migrations/`). Clients can only reach them through database functions that check the signed-in account. **Ask Claude** runs in the `dnd-advise` edge function (`supabase/functions/dnd-advise/`).

### Add it to your home screen

It installs like an app (no app store): on iPhone, open the link in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**; on Android, Chrome's menu → **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**). It opens full-screen with its own d20 icon and keeps the last copy for when the signal drops; the table itself always needs a connection. On iPhone the home-screen app keeps its own sign-in, so sign in once more there. `src/manifest.json`, `src/sw.js`, and `src/icons/` (drawn by `scripts/make-icons.py`) make this work.

### Who can start tables

Anyone can sign in (it's the UFC app's login), but only the group's DMs can **start** a table (`supabase/migrations/0010_dnd_hosts.sql`). Players join with the DM's link as usual. A stranger who signs up has no table, and every Ask Claude request needs a live table, so they can't spend Claude calls. A DM adds or removes DMs by email in the DM tab under **DMs** (the person must have signed in once). If you're not listed (no table was open when 0010 ran), add yourself once in the SQL editor: `insert into public.dnd_hosts (user_id) select id from auth.users where email = 'you@example.com';`. As a backstop, set a monthly spend limit for the Anthropic key in the Anthropic Console.

### Changing the hosted backend

- **Database:** add a new numbered file in `supabase/migrations/` and apply it to project `gkccophrdqtqcowmblre`. The Supabase SQL editor is the reliable route; MCP migrations that need a confirmation prompt can time out. Only ever create or change `dnd_` objects; the UFC tables share this database.
- **Claude prompt:** edit `src/advice.js`, copy it to `supabase/functions/dnd-advise/advice.js` (`npm test` fails until they match), then redeploy: `supabase functions deploy dnd-advise --use-api --project-ref gkccophrdqtqcowmblre`.
- **Allowed origins** for the edge function default to `https://andyrbrett.github.io` and `http://localhost:4173`. Override them with the `DND_ALLOWED_ORIGINS` secret.

## Local version (one laptop, same Wi-Fi)

The original setup still works without any accounts: a Node server on the laptop, with players on the same Wi-Fi.

### Requirements

- Node.js **22.13+** (uses built-in `node:sqlite`). No `npm install` needed.
- Chrome on the host laptop. Speech recognition needs internet access and microphone permission. Transcript text is shared with the room; no audio is recorded or stored.

### Run a session

```bash
npm run dev     # rooms in memory, cleared when the server stops
npm run table   # rooms saved to table.sqlite, so a restart mid-session keeps the room
```

1. Set the DM's mic as the laptop's input. In Chrome on the laptop, open `http://localhost:4173`, choose **Start a table**, then **Start listening**. Allow the mic and say a test line; it should appear under **Current scene**.
2. Send players the **Share player link** URL. It uses the laptop's LAN address; if the laptop has several connections, use the matching **Players** address printed in the terminal and keep the `?room=…` part.
3. Use **Character sheets and abilities** to edit the five profiles (The White Wizard, Arcturus, Logan Ninefingers, Dum Dum, Roan the Last Shadow). One ability per line: `tag | Name — cost, limits`. Tags: `combat`, `danger`, `explore`, `social`, `aid`, `any`, or `check` for anything not yet confirmed.
4. Host controls: pause listening, type an update, correct the latest transcript, or turn off auto-publish. Host control survives closing and reopening the tab on the host laptop.
5. **End room and delete session** when finished. Rooms close after eight hours with no new updates either way.

### Host from a phone (local)

A phone can't run the Node server, and phone browsers only allow speech recognition on `https://` or `localhost`, so a phone host should use the hosted version above. The same phone tips apply there:

1. On the DM's phone, open the app in **Safari (iPhone)** or **Chrome (Android)** and choose **Start a table**, then **Start listening**. Allow the microphone.
2. **Share player link** opens the phone's share sheet, so the DM can drop the link in the group chat.
3. Keep the app in front and the phone on power. While listening, the screen stays on. If the DM switches apps or the screen locks, listening pauses and resumes on return. If the phone blocks the automatic restart, tap **Start listening**.
4. Put the phone in front of the DM, not in the middle of the table, or it will transcribe the whole table. A clip-on mic plugged into the phone works best.

If phones can't load the page, the Wi-Fi may block device-to-device traffic (common on guest networks), or the laptop firewall may be blocking the server on the private network.
